Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how Sidelook Publisher (the "App") handles data when a Google account authorizes it. The App uses YouTube API Services; the Google Privacy Policy also applies.

1. Data we access

  • The authorizing account's own YouTube channel information (channel ID, title, branding settings).
  • The channel's own videos, playlists and their metadata, in order to upload, schedule and edit them.
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers) to evaluate video performance.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google.

2. How we use data

Data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos and to measure their performance. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose, and it is not used to train AI models.

3. Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with Google/YouTube as required by the API.

5. Storage and security

Tokens and statistics are stored on a private server accessible only to the owner and are never published. Rendered video files are deleted from the server after a short retention period.

6. Data of other users

The App does not collect or store personal data about viewers or other YouTube users. Aggregated, anonymous statistics provided by YouTube Analytics are the only audience data used.

7. Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time via Google Account permissions. After revocation, stored tokens are deleted, and you may request deletion of any stored data via the contact below.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.
